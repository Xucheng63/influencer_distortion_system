Detection pipeline

1
Content collection
Post history is crawled from Twitter/X, YouTube, and newsletters. Videos are transcribed via Whisper-class ASR; YouTube auto-captions serve as fallback.
All content is timestamped and archived before potential deletion — deletion rate is treated as an independent signal. Posts confirmed deleted are preserved and tagged separately.
2
Rhetorical pattern detection
A keyword and phrase model runs a fast, high-recall first pass across each post, tagging candidates across four distortion dimensions: significance inflation, anxiety manufacturing, novelty claims, and temporal distortion.
3
LLM-based classification
Ambiguous cases are passed to a language model that classifies distortion type and rates intensity on a 0–100 confidence scale. This step makes no claims about factual accuracy — only rhetorical framing pattern.
Confidence below 70% sends the post to the human annotation queue (see Research tab) rather than auto-flagging.
4
Temporal cross-reference
Linked content publish dates are extracted and compared against the post's claimed recency. A gap of 30+ days between original publication and the post date flags the post for temporal distortion scoring.
Original publish date is shown alongside the post on the Flagged posts tab. The largest detected gap in this account: 257 days.
5
Profile aggregation + consistency scoring
Per-account rolling statistics are computed across a 365-day sliding window. Alongside the four dimension rates, a consistency score measures week-to-week variance in distortion behavior — high consistency = more reliable signal, low consistency = noisier profile.
Consistency above 70 means the pattern is structural, not episodic. Gaming resistance: an account cannot selectively reduce its score without abandoning its core content strategy across weeks of posting history.
What this system does not do: It makes no claims about factual accuracy, does not judge whether something is objectively significant, and does not issue verdicts. The goal is reader calibration — not account punishment. The signal is always the pattern across posts over time, never any single claim.
